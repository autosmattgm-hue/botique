What to Include

Request a Piece

Clear details help us move faster, compare options confidently, and reduce back-and-forth before sourcing begins.

01

Brand and model

Send exact names, screenshots, colors, material preferences, and acceptable alternatives.

02

Budget and timeline

Share your ideal budget, maximum range, destination country, and when you need the item.

03

Sizing and condition

For clothing or shoes, add size conversions. For pre-loved items, specify condition expectations.
